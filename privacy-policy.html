Duty Manager — Privacy policy

Effective 28 September 2026

Duty Manager is an Android app for keeping a duty roster, attendance punch reminders, changeover alarms and leave records. It is published by Ratheesh Vijayan. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — duties, stations and their locations, punch times, reminders and leave records — is stored only in the app's database on your phone. There is no account and no server. The app has no internet access of its own, and contains no advertising or analytics.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location, including in the background, for one purpose: to notice when you arrive at or leave a duty station so it can remind you to punch in or out. This uses Android's geofencing through Google Play services. Your location is not stored as a history and is not sent anywhere by the app. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Android's settings; the roster and alarms keep working without it.

Backups

If you choose a backup folder, the app writes a daily copy of your data to it. That folder may be on Google Drive or another storage provider, and is covered by that provider's own terms. The app may also be included in Android's own backup to your Google account, if you have that turned on. Both are under your control.

Notifications and other permissions

Notification, alarm, full-screen, display-over-other-apps and Do Not Disturb access are used only so that reminders and alarms ring reliably and so studio mode can silence them. They do not give the app access to other apps' data.

Sharing

The app shares nothing with anyone. When you export a leave summary as a PDF or send feedback by e-mail, it is you who chooses where that goes.

Children

The app is intended for working adults and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all data it holds on the phone. Backup files you created are yours to delete.
